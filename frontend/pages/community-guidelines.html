Community Guidelines

Our shared expectations for healthy, vibrant, and respectful participation on JustBaddo.

1. Mutual Respect & Constructive Debate

Music criticism and album debates are welcome. However, personal harassment, tribalism, hate speech, or derogatory attacks aimed at community members will result in immediate suspension.

2. No Piracy or Leaked Materials

Do not post links to pirated MP3 downloads, unreleased studio leaks, or stolen content. JustBaddo is committed to ethical music archiving and respecting artists' creative work.

3. Factual Accuracy in Discography Claims

When sharing historical claims, chart records, or production credits, ensure they are substantiated by reliable public sources. Do not fabricate lyrics or present rumours as fact.

4. Reporting Inappropriate Content

If you encounter spam, offensive language, or copyright infringement, use the report action or reach out via our contact page.
