Privacy Policy

Last revised: November 2026

Notice: This policy is a demonstration draft for the JustBaddo Phase 1 frontend. Formal legal review will occur prior to production Django backend deployment.

1. Information We Collect

In the current development environment, JustBaddo stores user preferences, active queue items, and demonstration playlists exclusively inside your browser's local storage (localStorage). No personal data is transmitted to external tracking servers.

2. Cookies and Storage

We utilize local storage solely for preserving user interface preferences (such as audio volume, shuffle modes, and saved playlist structures).

3. Community Content

Posts and comments submitted to the community feed are displayed locally during the demonstration phase. Once connected to the backend database, public community contributions will be visible to other registered members.

4. Your Data Rights

You may clear your demonstration state, saved tracks, and playlists at any time by clearing your browser storage or using the clear action in the settings panel.
