About The Platform

Preserving A Legacy of African Music Culture.

JustBaddo is an independent digital fan and discography platform dedicated to chronicling the career, recordings, and global cultural footprint of Olamide Adedeji.

Verified Discography

Every track, album, release date, producer credit, and feature is verified from primary sources. We separate catalogue metadata from playback permissions with total integrity.

Fan-First Community

Music should bring people together. The Fan Arena, community discussions, and shared reflections enable fans in Lagos, London, New York and beyond to celebrate Nigerian rap heritage.

Independent Legal Status

JustBaddo is strictly an independent product. It is not an official management portal, nor is it commercially affiliated with Olamide, YBNL Nation, or EMPIRE.

Access to sound recordings is subject to legal licensing. Public playback during development is simulated through synthetic tones and non-infringing Web Audio APIs. We pursue direct rights and authorised integrations for production operations.
