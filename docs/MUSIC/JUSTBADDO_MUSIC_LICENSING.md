
# JUSTBADDO MUSIC LICENSING STRATEGY

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the music rights investigation and licensing strategy for JustBaddo.

JustBaddo intends to provide a music experience that allows fans to listen to Olamide's music within the platform.

This requires appropriate legal permissions.

The project must not assume that publicly available music, downloadable audio files or existing streaming subscriptions grant redistribution or public streaming rights.

## 2. Confirmed Licensing Decisions

| Area | Decision |
|---|---|
| Music access | Free initially, premium features later |
| Territories | Nigeria and international markets |
| Licensing route | Artist management and rights holders |
| Initial playback | Authorised provider integration |
| Long-term playback | Direct licensing and self-hosted streaming |

## 3. Rights That Must Be Investigated

### Sound Recording / Master Rights

These relate to the actual recorded version of a song.

The master recording may be owned or controlled by:
- A record label
- The artist
- A production or recording company
- Another authorised rights holder

### Musical Composition Rights

These relate to the underlying music and lyrics.

They may involve:
- Songwriters
- Composers
- Music publishers
- Publishing administrators
- Other authorised rights holders

### Performer Rights

Rights connected to performers and their recorded performances must also be considered.

### Artwork and Visual Rights

Album artwork, promotional photographs, music videos and other visual assets require separate consideration.

### Name, Likeness and Branding

Use of Olamide's identity in promotional or commercial contexts must be assessed separately.

## 4. Potential Licensing Contacts

The project should investigate:

1. Olamide's management or authorised representatives.
2. Relevant record labels.
3. Music distributors.
4. Master recording owners.
5. Music publishers and publishing administrators.
6. Songwriters and other composition rights holders where necessary.
7. Relevant collective management organisations.

Do not assume one party controls every recording.

## 5. Licensing Approach

Use two parallel routes.

### Route A: Artist Management

Contact the artist's authorised representatives to introduce JustBaddo.

Explain:
- The project's purpose.
- Its independent fan-community identity.
- Its music catalogue plans.
- The intended streaming experience.
- The target territories.
- Its free-first business model.
- The long-term vision.

Ask whether management can facilitate an introduction to the relevant rights holders.

### Route B: Rights Holders

Identify the parties controlling individual masters and compositions.

Request information about:
- Available catalogue
- Licensing authority
- Existing distribution arrangements
- Territory coverage
- Streaming rights
- Commercial conditions

## 6. Licence Requirements

The agreement should be reviewed by a qualified music-rights lawyer.

Areas to negotiate include:

- On-demand streaming rights.
- Interactive playback rights.
- Website and mobile-browser use.
- Commercial use.
- Free and premium access models.
- Advertising-supported use, if introduced.
- Permitted territories.
- Duration of licence.
- Renewal conditions.
- Royalty rates.
- Minimum guarantees, if applicable.
- Reporting requirements.
- Payment schedule.
- Audit rights.
- Artwork and promotional permissions.
- Track previews and snippets.
- Playlist functionality.
- Temporary caching or storage.
- User-generated playlist sharing.
- Takedown requirements.
- Termination conditions.

## 7. Territory Strategy

Initial target: Nigeria and international audiences.

Territorial permissions must be explicitly established.

A Nigerian licence does not automatically authorise streaming in the United Kingdom, United States, Canada, Australia or other countries.

Create a territory register for each licensed recording.

Suggested fields:
- Country
- Rights holder
- Licence reference
- Permitted use
- Start date
- End date
- Restrictions

## 8. Free and Premium Strategy

### Free Access

The initial product direction is free listening.

This does not remove the requirement to pay applicable royalties or comply with licensing conditions.

Possible free features:
- Catalogue discovery
- Authorised playback
- Album browsing
- Public playlists
- Basic listening history

### Future Premium Access

Premium features may include:
- Advanced playlist tools
- Personalised collections
- Enhanced listening statistics
- Exclusive community experiences
- Custom profile features

Any premium music access, ad-supported model or subscription offering must be expressly permitted by the relevant agreements.

Do not assume that premium access can be introduced under a free-use licence.

## 9. Provider Licensing

Third-party music providers must be evaluated separately.

An API or embeddable player does not automatically permit:
- Commercial redistribution
- Repackaging audio
- Removing provider branding
- Circumventing playback restrictions
- Unrestricted playback in every country

Obtain written clarification where the provider's standard terms are insufficiently clear.

## 10. Rights Register

Maintain a rights register containing:

- Track
- Album
- Master rights holder
- Composition rights holder
- Performer information
- Licence reference
- Territories
- Permitted uses
- Effective date
- Expiry date
- Restrictions
- Evidence documents
- Verification status

No track should be enabled for public streaming without the necessary rights verification.

## 11. Suggested Rights Statuses

- unverified
- contact_identified
- permission_requested
- negotiation
- licensed
- restricted
- expired
- withdrawn

## 12. Takedown and Withdrawal

JustBaddo must have a process for:
- Receiving rights complaints.
- Verifying the complaint.
- Temporarily restricting disputed content when appropriate.
- Removing content when legally required.
- Recording the action.
- Notifying relevant stakeholders.
- Preventing unauthorised re-upload.

## 13. Legal Considerations

The Nigerian Copyright Act 2022 is an important starting point for local rights analysis.

International distribution may trigger copyright and licensing requirements in multiple jurisdictions.

Professional legal advice is required before public streaming.

This document is a project planning document, not legal advice or a licence.

## 14. Licensing Completion Criteria

- Relevant rights holders identified.
- Authority to grant rights verified.
- Master rights addressed.
- Composition rights addressed.
- Performer rights assessed.
- Territories confirmed.
- Commercial model permitted.
- Written agreements executed.
- Usage reporting requirements implemented.
- Takedown procedure established.
